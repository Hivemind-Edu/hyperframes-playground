CONTENT_BRIEF:
Create a scenario video where a present-day person wakes up in ancient Rome with modern knowledge. Do not make it wish fulfillment. Show that ideas are useless without supply chains, measurement tools, sanitation systems, literacy, institutions, capital, political protection, and labor.
