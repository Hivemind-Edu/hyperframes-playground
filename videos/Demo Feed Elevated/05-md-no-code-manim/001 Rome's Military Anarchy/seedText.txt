CONTENT_BRIEF:
Explain why Rome's third-century Military Anarchy happened. Focus on the structural shift from dynastic or Senate-sanctioned legitimacy to army-manufactured emperors. Start with Severus Alexander's murder in 235 CE and Maximinus Thrax's elevation by the troops. Show that legions learned they could create emperors, that payroll and frontier crisis replaced bloodline, and that Diocletian's later power-sharing reforms answered a broken single-throne system. Keep it causal, concrete, historically grounded, and animated scene friendly.
