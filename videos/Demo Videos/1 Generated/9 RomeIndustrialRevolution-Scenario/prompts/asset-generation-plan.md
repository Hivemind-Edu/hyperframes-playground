# Rome Industrial Revolution Scenario - Asset Generation Plan

## Working Direction

This video should keep the viral scenario energy of the TikTok reference while making the educational point sharper:

> A modern engineer could impress ancient Rome with prototypes, but probably could not start a full Industrial Revolution alone. The missing pieces are fuel, precision machining, cheap energy, repeatable manufacturing, institutions, markets, and labor incentives.

Tone: serious educational with absurd character comedy. The fun should come from the skeleton engineer's reactions, Crocs, modern outfit, and animated physical business, while the facts stay accurate.

Format: portrait `392x768`, roughly `65-75s`, photorealistic, fast second-person scenario, word-by-word TikTok captions, hard cuts, time jumps.

## Iterative Asset Order

1. **Locked character reference**
   - Use `assets/characters/skeleton-engineer-character-sheet-v7-barely-translucent-skin-nb2-2k.jpg`.
   - This is the recurring skeleton engineer: green cap, normal-length green utility overshirt, white T-shirt, tan knee-length work shorts, green Crocs-style clogs, leather satchel, exposed skeleton arms and legs with a very subtle clear translucent sheen.

2. **2x2 Roman-world visual proof grid**
   - Generate four representative story frames in one image.
   - Goal: prove the character still reads correctly in cinematic Rome environments before generating all scenes.
   - The four proof beats should test:
     - Hook in Rome.
     - Workshop/precision bottleneck.
     - Steam prototype that impresses but does not scale.
     - Social/institutional bottleneck.

3. **Voiceover script**
   - Draft around 150-170 words for 65-75s.
   - Structure: hook, day/week/month/year jumps, escalating constraints, final systems insight.
   - Validate facts before image generation locks them visually.

4. **16-scene storyboard plan**
   - Convert the script into exact scene prompts, durations, caption chunks, motion direction, and SFX ideas.
   - Use the visual proof grid to decide the final aesthetic.

5. **Scene reference grid**
   - Generate a 4x4 grid of all 16 scenes at 2K or 4K.
   - Purpose: global consistency across Rome, lighting, camera style, and recurring character.

6. **Individual 16 scene keyframes**
   - Generate each final still from the 4x4 grid plus character reference.
   - Check for shirt length, cap, Crocs, translucent arms, no logos, no bad hands, no accidental modern text.

7. **Image-to-video clips**
   - Generate muted 3-6s clips per scene.
   - Favor camera drift, steam, water, forge fire, crowd movement, parchment motion, gears turning.
   - Avoid complex eating, face-touching, and high-risk hand closeups.

8. **Audio and HyperFrames assembly**
   - Generate ElevenLabs voiceover.
   - Transcribe for captions.
   - Assemble full-frame video clips, global audio, captions, scene gradients, and occasional educational emphasis overlays.

9. **Inspect and render**
   - Run `npx hyperframes inspect --json`.
   - Preview key frames and render only once the timing and captions are clean.
