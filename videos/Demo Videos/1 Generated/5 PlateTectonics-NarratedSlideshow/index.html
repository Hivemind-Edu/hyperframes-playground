In 1912, Alfred
Wegener presents a
radical theory: continents
plow through oceans
like icebreakers through
ice.
Wegener calls it
continental drift, pointing
to identical fossils
on opposite sides
of the Atlantic.
Physicists mock his
theory because he
cannot identify the
engine driving these
massive landmasses.
Scientific consensus agrees:
the deep ocean
floor is a
flat, featureless mud
plain.
For forty years,
Wegener's theory remains
dead and buried
in academic obscurity.
Without a mechanism
to move continents,
geology stalls in
a dead end.
In 1952, cartographer
Marie Tharp sits
at a draft
table in Columbia
University's Lamont Geological
Observatory.
She is barred
from research ships
because women are
considered bad luck
on ocean voyages.
Her colleague Bruce
Heezen works the
decks, collecting sonar
soundings across the
Atlantic.
Heezen sends raw
depth profiles back
to New York,
where Tharp translates
numbers into landscape.
Instead of a
flat plain, Tharp
discovers a massive
mountain range rising
from the abyss.
A mountain chain
that dwarfs the
Andes and the
Himalayas combined.
But Tharp notices
something far more
shocking: a deep
trench splitting the
center of the
ridge.
She recognizes it
immediately as a
rift valley.
A rift valley
means the Earth's
crust is pulling
itself apart.
Heezen dismisses her
discovery as 'girl
talk' that supports
Wegener's forbidden theory.
He orders her
to recalculate the
profiles, certain she
made an error.
Tharp plots the
data again.
The rift valley
remains.
Then, Heezen plots
the epicenters of
deep-sea earthquakes.
Every earthquake aligns
perfectly inside the
central trench.
The rift is
active, cracking the
planet open over
thousands of miles.
Geologist Harry Hess
of Princeton University
reviews this massive
underwater mountain system.
Hess realizes the
rift is not
just a crack.
It is a
wound that never
heals.
In 1960, Hess
proposes 'seafloor spreading'
to explain the
active wound.
The ocean floor
acts as a
massive conveyor belt,
carrying the continents
passively along.
Wegener's missing engine
is finally found.
The dynamic earth
is born.
You are studying
the system that
continually rebuilds our
planet.
